How events are scored▼

Score = (Proximity × 0.3) + (Quality × 0.7). Proximity comes from the walk to the nearest point on the canal: up to 5 minutes is 10, 10 minutes is 9, 15 minutes is 8, 20 minutes is 6.5. Anything farther is left out.

Quality starts as a judgment of the event on its own merits, then gets adjusted: a floor for venues that book reliably, a penalty for recurring nights (weekly −1.5, twice a month −1.0, monthly −0.5), and +0.6 for free events. A quality of 8.6 or more needs researched evidence, otherwise it's capped at 8.4. Open any card's "Why" to see its math.
